# Decision Analysis for Energy Investment

A decision analysis of three ways to invest in electricity generation, comparing a natural gas plant financed in two different ways with a solar PV farm.

## Overview

An energy company has to choose between building a natural gas plant (financed with green bonds or with a bank loan) and developing a solar PV farm, while electricity prices, demand and costs are uncertain. We modeled the decision end to end and looked at how the best choice changes with new information and with the decision maker's attitude to risk.

## What we did

- Built an influence diagram and a decision tree for 3 investment strategies and compared them with expected monetary value and risk profiles
- Checked dominance between the strategies and ran one way and two way sensitivity analyses on the key uncertain inputs
- Quantified the value of information, finding a 12.4% increase in expected value with perfect information and 8.3% with imperfect information about electricity prices
- Built utility functions for risk averse, risk neutral and risk seeking decision makers and showed how the preferred strategy changes for each

## Methods

Decision trees, influence diagrams, expected monetary value, risk profiles and stochastic dominance, one way and two way sensitivity analysis, value of perfect and imperfect information, utility theory.
